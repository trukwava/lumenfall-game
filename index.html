THE LAMPLIGHTER'S CODEX

Light reveals.
Darkness protects.

Defeat every shadow across six chambers to survive. Choose a relic after clearing each non-final chamber. You have 3 action points each turn. Move, aim, toggle the lantern or strike for 1 AP each, then end your turn to let enemies act.

  • Move: arrow keys / WASD, or click an adjacent tile.
  • Aim: Shift + arrows, or the four direction buttons.
  • Toggle: L or click the lantern switch. Turning off hides you from distant Watchers.
  • Strike: F or click an illuminated enemy. Only enemies touched by your lantern beam can be struck.
  • Rewind: Z undoes actions before you end the turn.
  • Commit: Enter ends the turn. Red warnings and dotted arrows preview exactly what enemies will do.
